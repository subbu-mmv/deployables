Butter Chicken

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup tomato
  • ½ cup cream

Procedure

  1. Marinate the chicken.
  2. Cook the chicken until browned.
  3. Add tomato and spices.
  4. Add cream.
  5. Simmer for 10 minutes.
