<details>
<summary>Butter Chicken</summary>

## Ingredients

- 500g chicken
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 cup tomato puree
- ½ cup cream

## Procedure

1. Marinate the chicken.
2. Fry the chicken.
3. Make the tomato sauce.
4. Add cream.
5. Simmer for 10 minutes.
